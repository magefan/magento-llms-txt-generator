## What Is LLMs TXT in Magento and Why It Matters?


Llms.txt is a file designed to guide large language models (LLMs) or AI crawlers on how to crawl, interpret and use your content. It's similar to how robots.txt guides search engine crawlers.

It helps AI models to understand:

* Which parts of your site can they access
* Which pages are important
* How your content should be interpreted
* What is your brand positioning and purpose

Since AI-powered search grows, llms.txt file becomes an important part of how your Magento store appears in AI-generated answers.

## Why Magento Stores Need LLMs TXT Now?

Search behaviour is changing. Users are actively getting answers from AI tools, like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, instead of traditional search engines. 

These AI-based models rely on structured and accessible content to retrieve and generate responses.

Without llms.txt guide:

* LLMs may ignore important pages
* Your content can be misunderstood or misrepresented
* You have no control over how your store appears in AI answers

## FAQs

### How is Magento LLMs TXT file different from robots.txt?

Magento llms.txt file and robots.txt file are completely different. Robot.txt controls what pages traditional web crawlers can access, while llms.txt focuses on giving AI system enough context about your brand and most important pages so they interpret and use your content to generate answers.

### Can LLMs TXT in Magento improve SEO?

There is no direct link between LLMs TXT and SEO since llms.txt doesn't improve traffic from traditional search results, which SEO is focused on. 

However, llms.txt improves SEO indirectly by making your content more visible for AI systems, which are part of the search experiences.

### What content should be included in Magento LLMs TXT?

It is recommended to include 100-200 links in the llms txt. Include the most important pages, such as products, categories, CMS content, and blog pages, that you want cited and visible in AI search.
